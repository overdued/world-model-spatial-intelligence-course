(PDF 1 original scan (https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/2hqmjazni0axuzlx4exkq/Computing-Machinery-and-Intelligence-Alan-Turing-SCAN.pdf?rlkey=xl38t9c5cra8jktqx1t30jcgu&dl=0), PDF 2 w/ minor OCR errors (https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/kivpl5gvueuty6lkwom8z/Turing-Computing-Machinery-and-Intelligence-1950-OCR-CLEAN.pdf?rlkey=p815ok7r5rhfviv9bcnbn9hre&dl=0)).
 Note about this reading: This was written in 1950 and has verbiage that today is considered controversial and biased; treat this more as an historical artifact rather than a modern-day text. It’s important to understand the origin of the “Imitation Game” (aka “The Turing Test”) with respect to the current thought around AI, particularly the prominent chatbot user experience.
