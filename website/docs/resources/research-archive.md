# Research Archive

Behind the polished tracks sits a research archive: the survey notes, structured metadata and design documents from which this course was synthesized. It lives in the repository, openly, so every curriculum decision can be traced back to a source.

## Ground rules

- **Traceability**: if a module makes a claim about "how CIS6280 teaches X", the receipt should exist in the archive.
- **Curate, don't copy**: the archive records links, structure and analysis — third-party slides and papers are never re-hosted.
- **Living document**: the archive is append-mostly; when the field moves (new video world models, VLA releases), new material enters the survey before it enters the curriculum.
